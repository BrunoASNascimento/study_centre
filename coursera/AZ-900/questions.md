Business description
A young entrepreneur is launching an online clothing store.

Challenges
They have limited initial capital (cost challenge).

They anticipate significant growth in the future (scalability challenge).

Cloud computing as a solution
Cost: Cloud computing eliminates the upfront costs of purchasing servers, software licenses, and IT infrastructure. The entrepreneur can pay-as-they-go for cloud storage and processing power, which aligns perfectly with their limited initial capital. This allows them to invest their resources in product development and marketing.

Scalability: Cloud services are highly scalable. The entrepreneur can easily increase storage space and processing power during peak seasons (e.g., holiday sales) and scale down during slower periods. This avoids the need to over-provision infrastructure upfront, saving money in the long run.

Flexibility: Cloud-based e-commerce platforms offer a wide range of features, including product management, secure payment gateways, and customer relationship management (CRM) tools. These features are readily available without the need for extensive IT setup or maintenance, allowing the entrepreneur to focus on running the business.

Limitations
Reliance on internet connectivity: Consistent internet access is crucial for accessing and managing cloud resources.

Vendor lock-in: Switching cloud providers can be complex if the e-commerce platform becomes heavily reliant on a specific vendor's services.

Cloud computing benefits
Reducing upfront IT costs.

Enabling easy scaling based on demand.

Providing access to pre-built e-commerce functionalities.

Business description
A large manufacturing company with on-premise data storage infrastructure.

Challenges
There's an ever-growing data storage need for production records and design files (storage challenge).

The current on-premise data storage infrastructure is expensive to maintain and lacks scalability (cost and scalability challenges).

Cloud computing as the solution
Cost: Cloud storage can be significantly cheaper than maintaining on-premise infrastructure. The company only pays for the storage it uses, and it doesn't have to worry about the costs of hardware upgrades, power consumption, and IT staff dedicated to storage management.

Scalability: Cloud storage offers virtually limitless scalability. The manufacturing company can easily add more storage space as its data volume grows, eliminating the need for frequent hardware upgrades or additional physical storage units.

Flexibility: Cloud storage allows authorized personnel to secure remote access data files, facilitating collaboration between geographically dispersed design teams and production facilities. This improves efficiency and streamlines workflow.

Limitations
There are security concerns whereby the company needs to ensure the cloud service provider has powerful security measures in place to protect sensitive manufacturing data.

Data egress (data being transported from a network to another location) fees: Downloading large data sets from the cloud can incur additional fees. The company needs to factor this into their cost calculations.

Cloud computing benefits
Scalable and cost-effective solution for data storage.

Enables remote access and collaboration on design files.

Simplifies data management and reduces IT overhead.
